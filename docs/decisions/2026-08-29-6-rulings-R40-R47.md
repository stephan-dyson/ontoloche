## R40 (Q45) — `propose_type(kind="predicate")` NEVER auto-approves: YES. Row 4c (INTERFACE amendment + contract id).

Belt-and-braces over `C10-09` / `C9-18`, and cheap: `propose_type` returns a `Proposal` for `kind="predicate"` regardless of the namespace's auto-approval policy, with warning `predicate_requires_review` (new value, through §5.12/R3). Pinned by a `C10` id in 4c.

## R41 (Q35 + Q44) — value-level conditions and ledger conditions are ONE Phase 3 decision, not two: YES to re-opening R22 for all gates at once, and Q44 joins it.
Three surfaces now reach for a value-level condition (`Consumer.gate`, edge gates R22, `Precondition` ACT4) and a fourth (ACT8, the ledger) asks a structurally different question that a precondition vocabulary would answer the same way. Phase 3's ingestion loop is the consumer that forces both; ruling them separately would produce two condition languages. Recorded as a Phase 3 must-decide alongside paging (R25) and tenancy (R24).

## R42 (Q36) — `projection`'s admission rule stays one host's convention: YES. Recorded.

## R43 (Q37) — `InvocationProvenance.imported_from` in v1, additive, default `None`: YES. Recorded, as R21 was.

## R44 (Q40) — `retention` is NOT a ninth key: YES, record and do not take.

## R45 (Q41) — `list_types` gets a `name` filter on the façade in v1: YES. Recorded for the ACTIONS build row.
`TypeQuery.name_in` exists at the adapter level; only the façade lacks it. Makes `type_active`'s negative cheap and complete (ACT6). Ships with the `C19` build, not before.

## R46 (Q42 + Q46) — `scope_conflict()` keeps its one-sentence return; `host_state` effects stay prose-identified: YES to both. Recorded.
Each would change a shipped shape for a case one fixture (or no backend) has produced.

## R47 (Q43) — the invocation ledger's paging routes to R25's Phase 3 decision, with the difference stated: YES.
The ledger is unbounded by construction where a vocabulary is bounded by curation; Phase 3's paging ruling must answer the ledger explicitly, not by analogy. Recorded on the Phase 3 must-decide list.
