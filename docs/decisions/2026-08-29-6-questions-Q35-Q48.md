## The two that decide what the venture sells

**Q39 — does the compliance arm sell enforcement, or evidence?** *(promoted to founder-visible by round 3)*
Should a deployment be able to declare that `record_invocation` **refuses** an invocation whose `gate_verdict` was `refused` — i.e. that the gate enforces? `ACTIONS.md` §4 says no, on the argument §2.5 gives twice: refusing to record an override destroys the evidence that the override happened. **The recommendation is to keep the no, and to notice that it is a product decision wearing an engineering question's clothes.** `VISION.md` §7's paid product is *"control documentation, ATO evidence packages, continuous monitoring, and someone accountable"* — an assessor asks for evidence, not for a mechanism — so the layer as built is *closer* to the revenue line than an enforcing gate would be.

**Q48 — should `VISION.md` §7 name the compliance arm's first deliverable?** *(founder's own document; recorded, not taken)*
Round 3's founder lens proposes: **a monthly signed override census**, run as `invocations(gate_verdict="refused", outcome="applied")`. §4's weakest **[Assumed]** is that *an operator who can see the override count will act on it*; the paid arm removes that assumption **by being the operator**. It is priced against staff time exactly as §7 argues the rest of the arm should be, and it can be sold before any of this ships.

## The one that decides how much of row #6 survives

**Q47 — cut §10 entirely?** *(founder-visible)*
Round 3's founder lens would cut the `projection` call, `ProjectionReport`'s fourteen fields, `consumers_at_risk`, ten numbered rules and the `reachability` key they force onto every family — on the grounds that §17.5 already concedes it is separable and §10.6 concedes it is *"the part of this document the venture's own customer deletes"*. **Not taken here: the brief for row #6 required it** (*"the tool-slot ceiling is a first-class design input"*), and a spec row does not delete a section its brief mandated on a reviewer's recommendation. **The counter-argument, for the record:** §10.1 is the one **[Observed]** measurement this row added, it is re-derived rather than cited, and §11.6 confirmed it live. Cutting §10 takes the family to **seven** keys.

## The gaps three surfaces now reach for

**Q35 — a value-level condition, at a third surface.** *(founder-visible)* `Consumer.gate` (contortion 11), edge consumer gates (**R22**), and now `Precondition` (**ACT4**). R17 was granted on **two** fixtures reaching for one value; this has three. **Recommendation: re-open R22 in Phase 3 for *all* gates at once, not for preconditions alone** — R22's own reasoning.

**Q44 — a precondition cannot ask about the invocation ledger.** *(founder-visible)* **ACT8.** Round 2's ingestion lens wrote the seven preconditions a real NYC ingest action needs and **one** was expressible. Idempotency — *"skip the 1,800 datasets I did yesterday"* — is the natural case, and §1 rules out an idempotency key by name in the same breath. **This is a different gap from ACT4** and nothing has ruled on it: R22 deferred *value-level* conditions, not conditions over this document's own store.

**Q38 — does R24's tenancy ruling survive actions?** *(founder-visible; gates the enterprise story)* An edge written for the wrong tenant is a wrong row; an action invoked for the wrong tenant is a wrong **effect**. Round 2 measured the cost of the workaround: a fixed-namespace effect declaration warns on **2,394 of 2,399 correct invocations**. **Recommendation: keep R24 for v0 and flag it as the ruling most likely to be revisited** — R24 is already explicitly provisional, and beacon's 2B harness is the evidence that decides it.

**Q45 — should `propose_type(kind="predicate")` ever auto-approve?** *(founder-visible)* Round 2's reviewer proposes it always return a `Proposal`, never a live `TypeEntry`. **`INTERFACE.md`'s to rule on, and the recommendation is yes**: a capability predicate is the one kind where an auto-policy approving is the kill row. The guard fixes landed in this row (`C10-09`, `C9-18`) make it belt-and-braces rather than load-bearing.
