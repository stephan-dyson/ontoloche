| [EDGES.md](https://github.com/stephan-dyson/open-ontology/blob/main/docs/specs/EDGES.md) | v0, unstable | **Typed relationships over the registry.** An edge family **is** a `kind="edge"` `TypeEntry` — not a fifth kind, not a predicate — so it inherits propose→approve, `resolve_type`, lifecycle and `consumers` unchanged, and EDGES adds no call of its own (the surface went 13 → 14 in row 3e, for `reinstate`). `equivalent_to` (**R7**) says *the same thing, kept apart* without licensing a merge; `neighbors(node, families, depth)` is capped at depth 2 (R13's consequence) with Rule K/U throughout; three adapter primitives. Three design tests driven through real CMS/NYC/beacon data; **eleven contortions recorded**; ten questions (Q12–Q21) open. |
